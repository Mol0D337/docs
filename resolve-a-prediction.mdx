---
title: "Resolve a prediction"
---

### Step 1 — Open predictions waiting for resolution

After the voting deadline expires, predictions move into the Waiting for Resolve state in the dashboard.

The API Owner can open the prediction and review all related market information before selecting the final outcome.

### Step 2 — Review prediction data

The dashboard provides detailed prediction statistics, including:

- total participants;
- Yes / No vote distribution;
- total volume;
- pool allocation;
- estimated payout distribution.

This information helps ensure accurate and transparent market resolution.

### Step 3 — Select the final outcome

Once the real-world event outcome is confirmed, the API Owner selects the winning result: Yes or No.

Prediction outcomes should always be resolved using reliable and publicly verifiable information sources.

### Step 4 — Automatic settlement and payouts

After the outcome is confirmed, the smart contract automatically executes the settlement process.

The system automatically:

- determines winning users;
- deducts platform fees;
- calculates proportional rewards;
- distributes payouts directly to connected wallets.

All calculations and transfers are processed on-chain through the Solana smart contract infrastructure.
